# Course Platform

An online course marketplace where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a written reason, to get their money back for a Purchase. It is pending until a person approves or declines it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund**:
Money actually returned to the learner, which happens only when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can currently be the subject of a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, not over the watch or completion limits, not a claimed team seat, and with no Refund Request already pending.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Chargeback**:
Not used in this domain. The word is reserved for a dispute raised through the cardholder's bank, which this platform does not model.
