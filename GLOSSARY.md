# Course Platform

An online course marketplace where learners buy courses and instructors publish them.

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with a written reason, to get their money back for a Purchase. It is pending until an Admin other than the learner approves or declines it; either decision is final.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, Reversal, Chargeback

**Quoted Refund Amount**:
The amount the learner is told they will get back when they raise a Refund Request. An approved Refund returns exactly this amount, never more or less.
_Avoid_: Partial refund, refund estimate

**Approval**:
An Admin's decision to grant a pending Refund Request. It creates the Refund and ends access to the course for everyone the Purchase paid for, including any team seats not yet claimed.

**Decline**:
An Admin's decision to refuse a pending Refund Request. It always comes with a Decline Note, and no money is returned.
_Avoid_: Rejection, denial

**Decline Note**:
The Admin's written explanation of a Decline, sent to the learner.

**Refund**:
Money actually returned to the learner, which happens only when a Refund Request is approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a Purchase during which the learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Purchase can currently be the subject of a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, not over the watch or completion limits, not a claimed team seat, and with no earlier Refund Request for it, whether pending, approved or declined. It governs only who may ask; an Admin deciding a Refund Request is not bound by it.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer

**Support**:
Not a separate role. Refund Requests are decided by Admins.
_Avoid_: Support agent, support team member

**Chargeback**:
Not used in this domain. The word is reserved for a dispute raised through the cardholder's bank, which this platform does not model.
